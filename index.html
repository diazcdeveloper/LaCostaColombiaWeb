Where We Are

From the shores of the Caribbean to the Pacific coast, we are present where the need is greatest. Explore the locations where our partners are leading sustainable change and bringing hope to the heart of Colombia, Use this map to discover the vibrant missions and foundations we empower in every region.

Mission

To strengthen and equip Christian leaders and local foundations across Colombia's Caribbean and Pacific regions. We serve as a strategic bridge—providing the resources, spiritual accompaniment, and visibility necessary for national ministries to lead sustainable transformation in their own communities through health, education, and the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Vision

To see the Colombian coast transformed by a vibrant network of self-sustaining local ministries. We envision a future where every community is led by empowered national leaders, and where the faithful service of the Colombian church is recognized and supported by the global body of Christ.

La Costa Colombia

We are a mission that supports missions and churches on the Colombian coast. Over the last 10 years, we have met great missionaries and pastors who were already working in their neighborhoods, and after witnessing the good work being done firsthand, La Costa Colombia began building spiritual and financial support relationships.

Our directors are based in the United States, from where they coordinate missionary trips, children’s dining halls, medical missions, and all kinds of events related to the comprehensive care of the community. All the rest of our collaborators working with La Costa Colombia are located in the various cities where we have been able to travel so far.

What We Do?

We collaborate with several congregations and missions in the cities of Barranquilla, Santa Marta, Sincelejo, and Buenaventura. Each of them carries out their own missions, focused on feeding and educating children, supporting and training young parents, and promoting community health and medical programs.

Additionally, we consider it essential to pray for and support the local pastors. Our team regularly meets with pastors online, and we occasionally organize retreats to provide them with rest, training, and encouragement.
