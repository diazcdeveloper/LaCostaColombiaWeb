Water and Words of Salvation.

Filtros de Esperanza' (Filters of Hope) walked through the streets of the most vulnerable neighborhoods in our communities. This time, our brothers and sisters came from Two Cities Christian Church and were a great blessing to everyone on the coast of Colombia. During this visit, our director, Pastor Jerry Sizemore, visited and met with our team in Barranquilla, Colombia. Pastors from across the city and leaders from many congregations shared time to share their progress and dreams.
