Mayo 23, 2025

Visit from our director, Pastor Jerry Sizemore

During this visit, our director, Pastor Jerry Sizemore, visited and met with our team in Barranquilla, Colombia. Pastors from across the city and leaders from many congregations shared time to share their progress and dreams.

Pastoral support and accompaniment is a priority of the mission, and our team has learned to add this to the hard work carried out in each of the supported projects. We thank God for the fulfillment of His purpose in us.
