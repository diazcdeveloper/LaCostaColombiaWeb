Ministries supported by La Costa Colombia

Barranquilla & The Caribbean Coast

Casa Firme & Construyendo Casa Firme

Led by Pastors Rogelio Torres and Elida Camargo, this partnership manages our flagship project: a Children's Feeding Center that provides daily nutrition and care. They also oversee our higher-education scholarship programs and micro-finance initiatives (interest-free loans) for small community businesses.

La Pesca Milagrosa

Under the leadership of Pastor Jose Jimenez, this ministry reaches six locations in Barranquilla and surrounding rural areas. We support their missionary journeys to hard-to-reach villages and provide scholarships for their youth leaders.

Fundepcar

Located in the Las Flores neighborhood, Director Carlos Gomez uses sports as a powerful tool to mentor at-risk youth and provide an alternative to the cycles of violence in the area.

Embajadores de Zion (Sincelejo)

Nestled in the "Peña de Horeb" area near Corozal, Pastors German and Juana Acosta lead efforts to support the elderly in rural towns, fostering entrepreneurship and providing essential medical and food supplies.

The Pacific Region: Buenaventura

Mahanaim Buenaventura

We support Sister Josefina De Avila, a dedicated missionary and leader. Our efforts here focus on a children's feeding center and providing theological support to strengthen the local church's doctrine and outreach.

Indigenous Outreach: La Guajira

Salud Hoy Foundation

Led by Dr. Tony Argote, this medical mission travels every two months to the desert regions of La Guajira. They provide free healthcare and life-saving medicine to the Wayuu indigenous communities, while simultaneously training local Christian leaders in the Word of God.
